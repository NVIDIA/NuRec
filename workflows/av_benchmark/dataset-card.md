## Dataset Description:

Neural-reconstructed dataset of 3D driving scenes. Each scene is about 20 seconds long and is stored as a USDZ file together with a front-camera video. The scenes are reconstructed with NVIDIA NuRec from the raw clips of nvidia/PhysicalAI-Autonomous-Vehicles, using 7 camera views (front-wide 120°, front-tele 30°, cross right/left 120°, rear right/left 70° and rear-tele 30°) and the top 360° lidar, by the NuRec AV Benchmark workflow. Every scene comes with its benchmark row: reconstruction quality on held-out frames, lidar fidelity, object-level quality, render rate and reconstruction time.

This dataset is ready for commercial/non-commercial AV only use.

## Intended Usage:

This dataset is intended to give AV developers hands-on experience of NuRec and 3DGUT reconstructions. Users can run tests and experiments for an AV system and train AI models that use camera and reconstructed data for internal development of autonomous vehicles and automated driving assisted systems using NVIDIA technology.
